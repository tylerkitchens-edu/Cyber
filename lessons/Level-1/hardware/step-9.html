Practice and Certification Exams

Learning Objectives

  • Complete the CompTIA A+ Core 1 practice assessment
  • Book a practice review with your instructor
  • Schedule and take the A+ Core 1 (220-1201) certification exam
  • Upload your score report, and know what happens if you need to retake
  • Apply test-taking strategies and time management

Your path to certification

Every CompTIA exam in this program follows the same five steps.

  1. Take the practice assessment. Sign in to CompTIA, open CertMaster Learn, and start the CompTIA A+ Core 1 Final Assessment. It has 90 questions and a 1 hour 30 minute limit. Take it once, in one sitting, closed book and with no notes, the same way you would take the real exam.

    Sign in to CompTIA (practice assessment)

  2. Book your practice review. Meet with your instructor to go over your practice results. Based on how you scored, you will either build a short study plan or be marked ready to test.

    Book a review with Jake

  3. Get your voucher and schedule the exam. When you are ready to test, your instructor sends you an exam voucher code. Use it to schedule your exam with Pearson VUE, CompTIA's testing provider, either online from home or at a test center. Confirm your test date with your instructor.

    Schedule your exam (Pearson VUE) What taking the exam online is like

  4. Take the A+ Core 1 (220-1201) exam. Up to 90 questions in 90 minutes. The passing score is 675 on a scale of 100 to 900.
  5. Upload your score report, pass or fail. When your instructor marks you ready to test, you get a personal upload link by email and on Discord. After your exam, use that link to upload your score report as a PDF, screenshot or photo. To find the report, sign in at CompTIA, then go to Manage Exams, My Exams, View Score Reports.
    • If you passed: congratulations! Once your score report is confirmed, you move on to Level 2.
    • If you did not pass: book a post-exam review with Jake and Tyler. Bring your score report: it lists the topics you missed, and you will use it to build a study plan before you retake. Book the post-exam review

Core 1 Exam Overview

Understanding the Exam Structure

  • The first 4-5 questions will be scenario or performance-based questions; all others are multiple choice

Performance-Based Questions (PBQs)

  • Simulated scenarios requiring hands-on solutions
  • Usually appear at the beginning of the exam
  • Worth more points than multiple-choice questions
  • Strategy: skip them at first, answer the multiple-choice questions, then come back

Multiple Choice Questions

  • Single answer vs. multiple correct answers
  • Situational scenarios
  • Direct knowledge testing
  • Process of elimination is key

Test-Taking Strategies

Time Management

  • Total time ÷ number of questions = time per question (about 1 minute each)
  • Save about 20 minutes at the end for the PBQs you skipped
  • Check the clock at 25, 50 and 70 minutes: by then you should be about a third, two thirds and all the way through the multiple-choice questions
  • Mark uncertain questions for review and come back to them if time allows

Question Analysis

  1. Read the entire question
  2. Identify key words
  3. Eliminate obvious wrong answers
  4. Consider the most practical solution
  5. Trust your first instinct
