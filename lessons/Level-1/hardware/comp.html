CompTIA CertMaster Learn is an online learning platform that provides:

  • Interactive lessons and practice questions for CompTIA certification exam prep
  • Performance-based learning objectives
  • Progress tracking
  • Mobile accessibility
  • Custom study plans

How to effectively utilize the certmaster learn

Comptia exam prep timeline

3 weeks out

  • Gather your materials: CertMaster Learn (lessons, practice questions and the practice assessment), the lesson pages in this course, and the free Professor Messer videos linked on them.
  • Join a study group or ask in the cybersecurity general channel on Discord.
  • Create a study schedule.

2 weeks out

  • Start the practice quizzes, and aim for 100% on each one. Repetition is key.
  • Keep a list of weak areas to review in your 1-on-1s with instructors.
  • Finish your first pass through all the exam objectives.

1 day before

  • Light review only.
  • Check your exam location or online testing requirements.
  • Focus on rest and mental preparation.

The CompTIA content and information can get a bit dry, or hard to digest. If you’re feeling unmotivated or stuck please reach out to your peers or an instructor! Make sure to participate in the weekly meetings, and schedule a 1 on 1 with an instructor if you need some assistance.
