Overview

This lesson teaches students about data protection strategies and compliance requirements, including encryption, data classification, regulatory frameworks, and legal obligations. Students will understand how to implement data protection measures, maintain privacy, and comply with standards such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS, ensuring organizational accountability and security.
