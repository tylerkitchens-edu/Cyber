Overview

This lesson focuses on analyzing vulnerability scan results to determine true risk. You will learn to validate findings, assess exploitability using CVSS scores and exploit databases, correlate vulnerabilities with threat intelligence, evaluate asset criticality and exposure, and prioritize remediation. The lesson also covers common vulnerability types including misconfigurations, unpatched software, weak credentials, and insecure defaults.
