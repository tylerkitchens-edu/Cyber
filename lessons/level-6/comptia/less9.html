Overview

This lesson focuses on the communication aspects of incident response. You will learn about incident declaration procedures, escalation paths, internal and external notification requirements, regulatory reporting obligations, status update cadences, post-incident reporting, root cause analysis documentation, and lessons learned facilitation. The lesson emphasizes clear, timely, and audience-appropriate communication throughout the incident lifecycle.
