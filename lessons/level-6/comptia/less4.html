Overview

This lesson examines how security operations teams continuously improve their processes and capabilities. You will learn about security operations center (SOC) maturity models, workflow optimization, automation and orchestration (SOAR), metrics-driven improvement, tabletop exercises, after-action reviews, and how to align security operations with organizational risk management objectives and service level agreements.
