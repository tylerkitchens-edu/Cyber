Lesson 4: Comparing System Architectures

CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-004)

Overview

Analysts now protect a mix of on-premises servers, cloud services, containers and connected industrial equipment. Each design changes what you can see, what can go wrong, and who is responsible for fixing it.

You will learn:

  • the security trade-offs of virtualization, containers, cloud-native services and APIs;
  • Zero Trust, SASE and hybrid cloud network designs;
  • why operational technology (OT, ICS, SCADA) needs different handling;
  • where encryption and data protection fit into each design.

On the exam this is mostly objective 1.1 (infrastructure, network architecture and critical infrastructure concepts).

Zero Trust and SASE

  • Zero Trust means "never trust, always verify": being on the company network earns nothing. Every request is checked for who the user is, the health of their device and what they are allowed to reach. Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) gives access to one application at a time instead of the whole network, as a traditional VPN does.
  • SASE (secure access service edge) delivers networking and security (ZTNA, secure web gateway, cloud firewall, data loss prevention) as one cloud service, close to wherever the user is.

Critical infrastructure: OT, ICS and SCADA

Operational technology runs physical things: power, water, factories. Industrial control systems (ICS) and SCADA systems that supervise them put availability and safety first. Devices may run for decades, can't be patched or rebooted on short notice, and can be disrupted by an aggressive scan. Protect them with strict segmentation and passive monitoring.

Check your understanding

A remote worker is on the company VPN, but still has to re-prove who they are and that their laptop is healthy before opening the HR app. Which model is this?

Zero Trust gives no trust for network location; every request is checked for identity, device health and permission.

Why are active vulnerability scans run very carefully on SCADA networks?

Availability and safety come first in OT. Prefer passive monitoring and scheduled, tested maintenance windows.

In the cloud, who is responsible for a storage bucket left open to the public?

Under shared responsibility the provider secures the platform, but the customer owns its data and settings.
