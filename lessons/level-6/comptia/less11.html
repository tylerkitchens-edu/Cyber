Overview

This lesson focuses on the analytical methodology for investigating potentially malicious activity. You will learn to analyze indicators of compromise (IoCs), distinguish between true positives and false positives, perform root cause analysis on alerts, map attacker behavior to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, identify lateral movement and persistence mechanisms, and document findings for escalation or closure.
