Overview

This lesson covers the full vulnerability response lifecycle including identification, classification, and management of vulnerabilities across enterprise environments. You will learn about vulnerability disclosure processes, responsible handling practices, asset inventory management, and how organizations prioritize remediation based on risk context, business criticality, and threat intelligence. The lesson also addresses patch management workflows, compensating controls, and exception handling.
