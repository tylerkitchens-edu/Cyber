Overview

This lesson covers the communication and reporting aspects of vulnerability management. Topics include vulnerability reporting formats, risk scoring communication, stakeholder-appropriate reporting (technical vs. executive), remediation tracking, SLA management, escalation procedures, and compliance reporting. You will learn to build effective vulnerability dashboards and present findings that drive action across technical and business teams.
