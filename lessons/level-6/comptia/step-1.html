You have completed all 14 CertMaster Learn lessons for CompTIA CySA+ CS0-003. It is now time to schedule and take your certification exam.

Visit CompTIA CySA+ Certification Page to learn more about the exam, and Pearson VUE to schedule your exam at a testing center or online.

Exam Details

  • Exam Code: CS0-003
  • Max Questions: 85 (multiple-choice and performance-based)
  • Duration: 165 minutes
  • Passing Score: 750 (on a scale of 100-900)
  • Exam Cost: ~$404 USD

Preparation Tips

  • Review all 14 CertMaster Learn lessons and complete any remaining practice questions.
  • Focus on performance-based question (PBQ) practice — these test hands-on analytical skills.
  • Domain 1 (Security Operations) and Domain 2 (Vulnerability Management) account for 63% of the exam — prioritize these areas.
  • Practice log analysis, SIEM queries, and vulnerability scan output interpretation.
  • Review the official CompTIA CS0-003 exam objectives to confirm coverage of all domains.
  • Take at least two full-length practice exams under timed conditions before scheduling.
