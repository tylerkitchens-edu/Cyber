Overview

This lesson covers the incident response lifecycle in depth. You will learn about preparation, detection and analysis, containment strategies (short-term and long-term), eradication, recovery, and post-incident activities. Topics also include evidence preservation, chain of custody, forensic data collection, incident classification and severity determination, and coordination with internal teams and external stakeholders during active incidents.
