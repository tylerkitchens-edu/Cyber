Overview

This lesson provides hands-on coverage of vulnerability scanning methods and tools. Topics include credentialed vs. non-credentialed scanning, agent-based vs. agentless approaches, internal vs. external scans, active vs. passive discovery, web application scanning, and infrastructure scanning. You will learn to configure scan policies, schedule scans appropriately, and interpret scanner output including false positives and scan coverage gaps.
