Overview

This lesson examines threat intelligence sources, types, and lifecycle management. You will explore open-source intelligence (OSINT), commercial threat feeds, information sharing organizations (ISACs), STIX/TAXII frameworks, and how to operationalize threat intelligence within a SOC. The lesson also introduces proactive threat hunting concepts including hypothesis-driven hunting, indicator-based searches, and the use of MITRE ATT&CK as a hunting framework.
